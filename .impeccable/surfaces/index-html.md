# THORAL Portfolio Surface Brief

- **Scope:** Primary showcase route (`index.html` / `src/App.tsx`)
- **Mode:** Experience
- **Audience & Action:** Creative directors, engineering leads, and prospective collaborators exploring interactive 3D and frontend work and initiating contact.
- **Constraints:** Zero fabricated biographical/client/contact claims (clearly marked configurable/conceptual); zero emojis (SVG only); full responsive adaptation from 320px to 4K + `prefers-reduced-motion` and WebGL fallback.

## Direction contract

THESIS: THORAL presents creative engineering as a precision-machined architectural monolith suspended between split obsidian and smoked-graphite studio planes, refusing the generic centered SaaS hero, glowing neon-cyberpunk clichés, and repetitive three-column portfolio card grids.

OWN-WORLD: Calibrated four-step dark zone architecture (`#0C0E12` abyssal carbon, `#12151B` matte obsidian, `#191E26` smoked slate, `#212731` floating architectural slab) divided by razor `1px` warm-ivory seams (`rgba(238, 232, 223, 0.08)`), sandblasted matte-black 3D anodized surfaces lit by cool rim light and incandescent forged-copper signal orange (`#E85D24`), set in self-hosted Urbanist bold/hairline weight-contrasted display type and Hanken Grotesk warm-bone editorial prose (`#EEE8DF` / `#ACA59B`).

STORY: Visitors immediately experience tactile 3D light, occlusion, and spatial choreography in the hero, move through a restrained editorial identity manifest, explore asymmetric conceptual case studies with interactive inspection, scan an architectural matrix of configurable capabilities, and close at a decisive contact terminal.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Split vertical and diagonal dark-plane studio stage framed by perimeter navigation (custom geometric THORAL SVG mark top-left, minimal chapter links + drawer trigger top-right, vertical `01–05` depth rail mid-left). Upper-left houses stacked bold/hairline `THORAL` / `ATELIER` display typography and a solid signal-orange primary CTA (`Explore Works`); center-right holds an interactive Three.js matte-black faceted sculptural monolith physically occluding a luminous burnt-orange orbital ring behind its upper shoulder, flanked by two asymmetric floating dark-slate specification slabs and a bottom scroll-depth indicator.
